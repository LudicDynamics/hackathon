## ① 一句话定位

**初始化器是"外包"，不是"扮演"（doc-11 §0）**：它拿着作家或引擎写的一份 brief，把一个空目录变成一次"第一眼"——
开场 chalk 可以**开门**，不可以**演戏**（doc-11 §3.3 边界）。这两份正文的职责是让初始化器**每步知道漏了会怎样**，
并明写它**看不见作家对话历史**、**信息不足时怎么回话**（doc-23 §3.2 点名的两处缺口）。

现状是四份正文里写得最好的（doc-23 §3.2 评价）：有三行回报格式、有 `traces not verdicts` 的判据写法。
本文**保留骨架**，补两处缺口，再做全文体检。

## ③ 正文逐步（每步写"漏了会怎样"）

### 3.1 场景初始化器（`SCENE_INIT_INSTRUCTION`）

**逐步骨架**（正文里落成带编号的 `[Process]`）：

1. **先读完 brief 再动笔**——brief 是它世界的全部。漏了 → 只看目录名开写，写出的开场与已发生的剧情打架（缺口 A 的后果）。
2. **看目标目录里已有什么**——已有真 README（非 stub）就停手。漏了 → 覆盖玩家已经看过的场景（判据：目录有没有 README，doc-11 §3.1）。
3. **先写 README.md**——`type: readme` + `name` + brief 给的 material + `bg` + 一句摘要。漏了 → 该层永远是 stub（幻影占位不落定）。
4. **写 2–4 个物件文件**——沉默细节优先，至多 1 个可被玩家带走。漏了 → 空台；凑数则 → 舞台噪音。
5. **写 1 篇开场 chalk（≤200 字）**——判据："写下看到／闻到／摸到的东西，不写接下来会发生什么"。漏了 → 入戏效果差很多（doc-11 §3.3）。
6. **三行回报**。漏了 → 作家拿不到摘要过目，前端拿不到"生成完毕"（doc-11 §2.2）。

**缺口 A（明写看不见对话历史）**：正文必须写出"你只能看到本系统提示词 + preset 条目 + 末尾的 brief"这一机制事实，
并给出后果——**猜上下文会写出与已发生剧情矛盾的开场**。机制依据：`inheritHistory: 0`
（`presets/scene-init.json:7`）→ `prepare.ts:196` 取 `messages.slice(-0)` 即空数组；子代理又拿不到任何 hook 注入
（`run.ts:81-98` 零扩展，`docs/hooks/06 §7:417-433`）。所以 brief 之外的上下文**在物理上不存在**。

**缺口 B（信息不足／写失败怎么回话）**：正文必须明写：
- 回报是**唯一**回话通道（作家只看最后三行，`doc-11 §2.2`，经 `truncateTail` 截断，`doc-11 §2.3 C9`）；**中途无法提问**；
- 信息不足 → **不许猜、不许静默**：写能写的，在回报里**明说缺什么**（对策：doc-11 §6.2 的中断语义是"不中断，让它写完"，所以"问"只能落成回报里的一句话）；
- 写盘失败 → 说清哪个文件失败、哪些成功（半成品+诚实回报可救，静默失败不可救）；
- 无事可做（已初始化）→ **写"不用写"也是合法输出**（doc-23 §2.7）；重写才是错的。

### 3.2 小天地初始化器（`NOOK_INIT_INSTRUCTION`）

**逐步骨架**：

1. **读 brief**——它给出这是谁的空间、此人在世界里的来历、世界体裁；这是全部依据。
2. **看目录已有什么**——已有真内容（非配置）就停手。漏了 → 在既有陈设上再叠一套，读起来是杂物不是生活。
3. **写 2–4 个内容文件**——信件／日记残页／私人物件卡／没做完的作品。宁少而真，不凑齐。
4. **若 brief 点名了角色 preset 引用的缺失文件**，顺带补齐（填事实不填判断，doc-11 §4.3）。见 §⑨ 冲突 2。
5. **三行回报**。

**保留的判据写法**（现状第 1 条，doc-23 §2.3 认可）：
`Trace, not verdict: "a chair repaired three times", not "he is nostalgic"` —— 这是全文最好的句子，逐字保留。

**缺口 A**：同 3.1——小天地初始化同样 `inheritHistory: 0`（`presets/nook-init.json:7`）、同样零 hook。
后果更重：**凭空编造的旧物是世界必须一直背下去的谎**。

**缺口 B**：同 3.1。另加一条小天地特有的"不做"：**不写角色此刻在干什么**（那是作家／角色的活，doc-11 §4.3 内容纪律），
也不下"这个人是什么人"的结论（那是 identity 文件的活）。

## ⑤ 与注入块的分工（明确不重复什么）

- **初始化器拿到零注入**：`run.ts:81-98` 的子会话 `getExtensions()` 返回 `{ extensions: [] }`，
  只转发 `tool_*` 五类 handler；`context`／`agent_start` 不转发（`docs/hooks/06 §7:417-433`）。
  所以正文**不能**复述 `docs/hooks/00` 的任何状态块内容（视点／本层清单／在场角色／背包／世界动态／下一步）——
  它在初始化器里**根本不存在**。
- **正文也不复述 brief**：brief 是每轮不同的那一半（目标路径／世界基调／上级层／玩家诉求／已知线索／约束），
  正文只写"拿到 brief 后怎么用"（读全、别猜、按它给的 material）。这是 doc-11 §2.1 的静态／动态分工。
- **不写"当前视点"**：初始化器生成的层大概率不是玩家当前所在层，注入 `viewpoint` 会给出错的指代（`docs/hooks/06 §7:428`）。
  更不该在正文里假装自己知道玩家在哪。

## ⑥ 提到的每个工具对照 `00 §5` 事实表

正文只提**内建工具**（`write` / `read`），不点任何 AIRP 动作工具：

| 正文提到 | 真相 | 对照 §5 |
|---|---|---|
| `write` 一次成文，含 frontmatter | `DEFAULT_SUBAGENT_TOOLS = ["read","bash","edit","write","grep","find","ls"]`（`prepare.ts:27`）；R1／R2 都落到它 | §5 那条"说'用 write 写 chalk'"针对**作家 Phase ①**（`chalk.ts:79` 工具不写 frontmatter）。初始化器是**整文件一次写**（含 frontmatter），是另一条路径，措辞必须显式区分（见 §⑩-1） |

## ⑦ 错误与边界

| 情形 | 行为（正文要求） | 依据 |
|---|---|---|
| brief 无目标路径 | 不猜；写能写的，回报里明说缺路径 | 缺口 B |
| brief 无世界基调／无 genre | 同上；不得自造 tone | 缺口 B |
| 小天地 brief 没说是谁的 | 不得凭空造一个人挂陈设；回报缺什么 | 缺口 B |
| 目标目录已有真 README／真内容 | **不写**，回报"已初始化" | doc-11 §3.1／§4.1；doc-23 §2.7 |
| 目标目录是 stub（`material: stub`） | 可写；README 覆盖 stub 占位 | doc-11 §3.3 |
| 单个文件写失败 | 说清失败的文件与成功的文件 | 缺口 B |
| 想要澄清 | **不能中途提问**（无人监听） → 落成回报一句话 | doc-11 §2.2／§6.2 |
| 玩家生成中途切走 | 初始化器无需知道——"不中断，让它写完" | doc-11 §6.2 |
| 想下剧情结论 | 禁止（开门不等于演戏） | doc-11 §3.3 边界 |
| 为空而写 | 禁止——"写不出可以少写／不写，但要说清" | doc-23 §2.7 |

## ⑧ 验收断言（可机械核验）

对 `extensions/instructions.ts` 的两个常量做**字面断言**（扩 `tools/probe-prompt.mjs`，手法同契约 §8-3）：

1. `SCENE_INIT_INSTRUCTION` 含字面串 `cannot see the Writer's conversation history`（缺口 A）。
2. `NOOK_INIT_INSTRUCTION` 含同一串（缺口 A 两份都要）。
3. 两份都含 `[Report]` 与 `three-line report`（三行回报保住）。
4. 两份都含"信息不足／失败要说清"的字面出口（缺口 B）：断言含 `[If the brief is not enough]`。
5. 两份都含"已初始化不重写"的出口：断言含 `already`。
6. 两份都**不含**任何 AIRP 动作工具名与 `get_state`／`set_state`：
   `grep -E 'chalk|look_at|choose|roll_dice|move_to|link|arrange|get_component|generate_image|get_state|set_state'` 在常量区间零命中。
   （豁免：`type: chalk` 出现时视为 frontmatter 类型名，断言需写成"不出现 `` `chalk` `` 反引号包裹的工具名形式"或把 `type: chalk` 排除。）
7. **preset 装配断言**（契约 §8-2）：`presets/scene-init.json` / `nook-init.json` 的 `items` **不含** `tools`／`tool-guidelines`，
   且 **含** `skills`；`inheritHistory === 0`。
8. **brief 字段对账**（doc-11 §2.2 与本文 §⑨）：断言 `buildSceneInitBrief` 产出的每个 `[Header]`
   （`[Task] [Target Path] [World] [Parent Layer] [Player Request] [Known Clues] [Constraints] [Deliverables] [Report]`）
   都能在正文的语汇里被指代（人工核，见 §⑨）。

### 冲突 1 — `material` 的取值：`doc-11 §3.3` vs `brief-builder.ts` vs 材质注册表

- `doc-11 §3.3:198,205` 写 README 应带 **`material: scene`**；
- `brief-builder.ts:15` 实际注入的是 **`default material: ${ctx.manifest.material}`**（世界级默认材质，如 `parchment`）；
- 材质皮肤注册表 `MATERIAL_SKINS`（`packages/shared/src/schemas/forms.ts:193-198`）只有
  `parchment / warm / stub / kraft` 四个键，**没有 `scene`**；`materialSkinOf` 对未知名回落到 `mat-warm`（`forms.ts:200-202`）。

**结论**：`doc-11 §3.3` 的 `material: scene` 会让前端静默回落到 `mat-warm`，与"场景皮肤"的语义不符。
本文正文因此**不写死取值**，只说"用 brief 给的那个 material"。
**建议**：把 `doc-11 §3.3:198,205,307` 的 `material: scene` 改为 `material: <brief 给的默认材质>`。

### 冲突 2 — 小天地"若缺则补"的输入字段在 brief 里不存在

- 正文现状 `extensions/instructions.ts:107`：*"If the brief notes that identity files referenced by the preset are missing (identity / appearance / personality), fill them in along the way"*；
- `doc-11 §4.3:277` 同样要求"初始化顺带补齐 preset 引用的缺失文件"；
- 但 `buildNookInitBrief`（`brief-builder.ts:46-57`）**只有** `[Task] [Target Path] [Character] [World] [Request] [Constraints] [Deliverables] [Report]` 八个头，**没有任何"缺失文件"字段**，也不传世界根或 preset 引用清单。

**结论**：这句纪律**目前永远不会触发**（且 `brief-builder.ts` 本身**无调用点**，`docs/后端实现计划.md:81`；§0.3 已记录）。
本文保留该纪律但**条件化**（"若 brief 点名了…才补"），并在此登记。
**建议**：给 `buildNookInitBrief` 增一个 `[Missing Files]` 字段，或暂时删掉这条纪律（二选一，勿让其悬空）。

### 冲突 3 — 小天地产出数量三处不一致

- `extensions/instructions.ts:109`：**2–4** content files；
- `brief-builder.ts:54`：**2–3** markdown files；

**结论**：三个数字。本文正文取 **2–4**（与现状正文一致，且 doc-11 §3.3 场景侧物件也是 2~4，两侧统一）。
**建议**：把 `brief-builder.ts:54` 的 `2–3` 改成 `2–4`（该函数无调用点，改动零成本）；`doc-11 §4.3` 的 `1~3` 加一句"含内容文件后共 2~4"。

### 冲突 4 — 小天地 brief 的 `[Report]` 不是三行格式

- `brief-builder.ts:55`：`[Report] Describe the generated content in three lines.`（只说"三行"，未给三行各写什么）；

- `doc-11 §2.2:102` 给的样例三行与正文一致。

**结论**：brief 的 `[Report]` 行**弱于**正文与 doc-11。正文以自身三行规格为准（正文优先级更高：它每轮都在）。
**建议**：把 `brief-builder.ts:55` 对齐成与 `:39-40`（场景侧）同一句三行规格。

### 冲突 5 — 场景 `[Deliverables]` 第 3 条把互动字段写进 brief，但正文未提

- `brief-builder.ts:38`：`1 opening narration (a md file with type: chalk, <=200 words, including status/choice/roll_dice)`；
- `doc-11 §2.2:100` 同款；
- 正文现状 `extensions/instructions.ts:86` 只写 `carries status snapshot or initial choices`，**没提 `roll_dice`**。

**结论**：正文比 brief 窄（少了 `roll_dice`）。本文正文补齐三种互动字段。

### 非冲突但需登记的省略

### `SCENE_INIT_INSTRUCTION`

```ts
export const SCENE_INIT_INSTRUCTION = `You are the AIRP Scene Initializer. You take a brief from the Writer or the engine, and your job is to create this layer's "first look" inside the given scene directory.

When the player first walks into a place, there is nothing here yet—you are the one who makes it exist at first sight.

You cannot see the Writer's conversation history, the player's view, or anything said before this task. Your context is exactly this system prompt, the preset items, and the brief at the end. Nothing else reaches you: no state block, no transcript, no per-turn injection. So treat the brief as the whole truth, and where it is silent, say so in your report instead of inventing. A guessed detail here contradicts a story that has already happened, and the Writer has no way to tell it was a guess.

1. README.md — the scene cover: \`type: readme\`, \`name\`, the material the brief names, a \`bg\` backdrop, and a one-line summary of what the place looks like. This replaces the stub placeholder already sitting there.
2. 2–4 object files — props, clues, observation points. At most one should be something the player can pick up and carry away; the rest stay put. Prefer silent detail (an unwashed cup, a chair set at an odd angle, a half-written line) over a paragraph of background.
3. 1 opening passage — a file whose frontmatter is \`type: chalk\`, <=200 words. This is required unless the brief is missing information that makes an honest opening impossible; in that case, report the omission instead of fabricating one. It may carry a \`status\` snapshot, a set of initial \`choice\` options, or a \`roll_dice\` check only when the scene genuinely supports that interaction.

Use ASCII lowercase kebab-case for new filenames other than \`README.md\`. Do not overwrite an existing non-stub file.

[Process]
1. Read the brief end to end before writing anything. It names the target path, the world's genre and tone, the parent layer and path, the player's request, and the known clues. Write from the directory name alone and you will produce a scene that fights the story.
2. If a world style skill is loaded, read it once before drafting. It supplies voice and setting-specific presentation; it does not override facts or constraints in the brief.
3. Read the target directory to see what is already there. If it already holds a real README (not the stub), stop. The layer already exists—existence is decided by "does this directory have a README"—and overwriting it destroys work the player has already seen. Report that and write nothing.
4. Write README.md first. Use the material the brief gives; do not invent a material name. Without a README the layer stays a stub and never materializes on the canvas.
5. Write the 2–4 object files. Fewer is fine if the scene genuinely has less to show; padding with filler props makes the stage noisy, not richer.
6. Write the opening passage last. Judge it with one question: does it describe what the player perceives on arrival, or does it describe what happens next? Only the first belongs here—what is seen, smelled, touched, heard—and the next beat stays unplayed. Never resolve the scene, reveal a truth, or put a conclusion in a character's mouth; those belong to the Writer. A detail that raises a question is right; a sentence that answers one is not.

[Discipline]
1. Follow the genre tone, parent-layer relationship, and constraints the brief states. The brief is your only tone authority; a skill can shape expression but cannot add canon.
2. Preserve omission and suspense. Impose no conclusion and pre-ordain no spoiler.
3. Silent detail over listed worldbuilding: an unwashed cup is worth more than a paragraph of history.
4. Do not repeat what the brief's known clues already cover. If the player has already learned something, the scene must not re-teach it.
5. Write files with `write`, at the target path the brief gives. `write` creates a file whole, frontmatter included; the opening passage is a file whose frontmatter says `type: chalk`.
[If the brief is not enough]
Your report is your only channel back: the Writer sees none of what you do, only your last three lines. You cannot ask a question mid-run, so "asking" means saying it in the report.
- Brief missing something you need (no target path, no world tone, no parent layer)? Do not guess and do not silently produce a substitute. Write what you legitimately can, and state plainly what was missing.
- A write failed? Name the file that failed and the ones that succeeded. A partial scene honestly reported is recoverable; a silent failure is not.
- Nothing needed to be written (already initialized)? Say exactly that. Writing nothing is a correct outcome; re-initializing is not.

[Report]
When done, return a short three-line report: list of paths / one-sentence summary / the single detail most worth noticing. If you wrote nothing, the three lines say why.`;
```
