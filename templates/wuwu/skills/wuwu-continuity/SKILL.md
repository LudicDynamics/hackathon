# Fogwharf continuity

## Core mystery

The crewless ship is a decoy. A young lord carrying a family ledger entered through the abandoned tax tunnel beneath the lighthouse. Old Mo killed the light for twelve minutes to guide that boat, unaware that a second boat of pursuers followed. Vera found a painted crest pin in the rudder. Silver Kite knowingly remained in public with an empty case as a decoy.

## Generation grammar

- Grow the world through harbor districts, workshops, towers, tunnels, walls, and islands.
- Every new folder is a traversable place with a README and at least one Chalk plus one usable clue.
- Make tools, badges, keys, oil, rope, bells, maps, and crests physically useful.
- Reveal the second boat only after the player connects three independent clues.
- Each step toward the truth extinguishes one town light in narration. Do not invent a numeric HUD.
- Silver Kite is terse and truthful; Vera jokes around facts; Old Mo tells the truth only at the top of the lighthouse.
- Beyond the fog grows according to whom the player has alerted.
