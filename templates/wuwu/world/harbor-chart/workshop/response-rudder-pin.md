Vera leans her violet arm against the bench and snorts a laugh at the badge. “Straight to the useful question. Good.”

She taps the drawer with one metal finger. “The pin was swapped, not lost. That matters. The new one is mine; the old one wore a crest under the grime. If you want the truth, look where the old metal came to rest, not where the wheel still turns.”
