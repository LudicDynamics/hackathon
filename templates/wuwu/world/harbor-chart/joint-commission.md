The knight offers thirty coins: find the ship’s cargo, and ask nothing of its origin.

The mechanic offers five: find the rudder pin that went missing.

An anonymous hand offers fifty: learn why the lighthouse went dark for twelve minutes. The handwriting was disguised by a left hand.
