Percentile dice: hover this Chalk and choose **Roll the Dice**.

Two D10s: left ×10, right ×1. A double zero means 100.

The final score stays on this Chalk. Start a fresh save to roll again. Ask the Writer to narrate the outcome after the roll if you want a story consequence.
