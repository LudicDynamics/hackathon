title: Why Edith changed the address
portable: true
---

This morning, Edith changed the novel's address to a non-existent street and handed it to the editor.

“If someone tried to go there, they'd realize such a street doesn't exist. I wanted to find out who would be troubled and ask about it.”

This is the method she thought of to investigate. We haven't found the culprit yet. We can verify from the editorial records who read that manuscript next.
