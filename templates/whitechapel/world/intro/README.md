You are **Sherlock Holmes**. Rain threads the window; your avatar is already reflected in the dark glass.

Watson has kept a frightened patient's secret for three days. This morning, the third crime occurred exactly as her unpublished illustration predicted. The fourth plate goes to press at noon.

He cannot betray his promise alone. He cannot let the picture become another murder. So he has brought the choice to you.

Fold this Chalk, take the ultramarine brass cap from the desk, and cross into the London case map.
