# World Execution Rules

This is a play that actually updates files. Do not declare success with dialogue alone. First, read sources of actions, current location and parent's README, intents, related items, character memories, and provided visitation history. README lists location and known exits, Chalk shows current choices with generation intent, this skill holds world invariants. Intents are directions for authors, not new engine commands.

## Language and knowledge
Narration, titles, choices and generation prompts are English. Filenames, directories and character IDs stay stable lowercase English kebab-case; never use a display name as a path. Examples: world/harbor-chart/beyond-the-fog/lighthouse-road/README.md, player/introduction-letter.md, characters/nanami/memory.md. Preserve README.md, SKILL.md, world.json, preset.json and existing asset paths. Give new Chalk a stable English path and English title and body. Characters know only what they witnessed or were told. Never turn secrets the player has not encountered into memories in an epilogue.

## Completing Actions
Each choice returns exactly one concrete change. Picked-up items move; reading does not move them. README doesn't move. Do not treat people as tools. Submissions are references, not consumption. Confirm paths and player meanings. Choosing does not substitute unsaid inference, plans or confessions. Draft substitute writing only on player request and confirmation.

Before execution, list files to create, update, or release. Write one passage fully, reread all files. If conditions unmet or tools fail, report deficiencies without releasing materials. Retry reads existing results, fixing only gaps. Prevent double rewards or spending. Do not create new world state files; keep facts in flat status.data and corresponding texts.

## Judgments
Before rolling, show target, needed items, uncertainty reasons, formulas, success/failure criteria, probabilities, major success, failures and risks, and option to cancel in Chalk. roll_dice must be YAML multiline. Inline {type:...} doesn't support rewriting results. expect uses quotes. Submission/entrance don't call roll_dice alone. Player rolls seeing conditions. Redo if conditions change. Dice don't decide truth or consent. Don't erase existing results or reroll free.


## Creating New Locations
Make one subfolder only within parent's README and Entrances. Don't expand town by checking ordinary rooms. Write a brief phrase at entrance, then README, two meaningful items, chalk with actions, and return gate. Include a note introducing one new inhabitant if needed, with name, job, possible words. Don't make conversation buttons for unregistered characterId. Author plays that encounter.


Do not consider README existence alone as complete. Verify child's README, two items, Chalk, and gate; fix missing. Don't redraw places or people on revisit. Call generate_image once after text completion, requiring real background matching time, light and objects. Write returned real asset only to README's bg. Image failure is text not failure; on failure say image absent, no auto-retry. Remove old bgVideo if incompatible. Playable with finished text first. 1–2 minutes are production budget, not generation time.

## From Verbal Statements to Deductions and Small Plans
First, ask "Who do you suspect? Based on which records or responses?" Summarize the statements briefly into player/deduction.md and confirm if they’re acceptable. Then ask "How do you want to verify this?" and summarize into player/operation-plan.md. Do not require two blank compositions. You can propose one idea from known tools, people, or places, but clearly mark it as a suggestion before adoption. Self-written documents are also accepted.
Deductions rely on observation and causality; plans are understood by subject, place, and action. Do not require the exact number of evidences or full item completion as a correctness gate. Question only one omitted point at a time; do not demand all items at once. Memorizing correct phrases isn’t necessary. Known conveniences are in world/london-map/unnamed-street/meeting-options.md. Do not ambush fictional addresses. Agree on real places like courtyards, get actual responses from intermediaries and targets. Do not use the patient themselves as a decoy without consent.

Movement to nonexistent streets stops at confirming maps or directions; refusal by individuals is enacted as refusal. Do not teleport to nonexistent places or fabricate consent.

## Even Incorrect Attempts Become One Act’s Result
After execution, choose one result based on actual deduction, plan, or action. The fixed culprit does not change. Failure is story-internal and not due to file incompleteness or API error.
- Chasing or confronting a wrong person: titled "Suspected person provides next clue." The suspect hesitates to cooperate. Their real documents and responses reveal a single contradiction to player interpretation. Example: Questioning Blackburn shows he tried to hide not the receipt time but responsibility for forwarding unreviewed corrections. It allows tracking to the receipt record’s recipient. Do not create alternative culprits or ultimate alibis.
- Executing an impossible plan, e.g., "Taking Edith to a nonexistent address": titled "Not a meeting place." The route is checked but not found; the attempt ends here. If Edith is present, she explains; if not, actual correction copies or maps show the fake address was a trap to observe reactions, not an invitation. Do not appear absent persons without consent. Ignoring her intent may cause hesitation to cooperate. Pure movement failure results in only one miss; do not add injury, confiscation, or death artificially.
- The suspicion is valid but the trap failed: titled "What was preserved and what was lost." Only truly saved persons or items count as success; differences with missed contacts or evidence are shown. The target only guards if aware of surveillance or invitation. Do not let uninformed targets see the plan.
- The event was actually prevented: titled "Person not at fourth." Show how main office’s observation and placement halted the attack and who reacted how. Even wrongful suspicion with successful protection counts as partial success; do not claim innocent arrests as successes.

Write titles and final sentences on-site. Each result is about 200–350 English characters summarizing your action’s reaction → unexpected perception → cost paid / protected → next checkable point as one act. Rewards for failures are not correct names but discoveries of missed relations. For unread documents, roleplay their acquisition or presentation; for read ones, point out misinterpretations without fabricating new evidence. Cite sources with stable real paths in remaining-object.md. Use real locations if physical items moved to player/. Do not multiply the same clue repeatedly.
