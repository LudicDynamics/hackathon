# The Cabin

A small, closed timber room in the present day. Rain touches the roof. You wake already seated on a chair at a bare wooden table. On the table are a sealed envelope and a black smartphone. Across from your seat, a single closed door has a working handle; it is not locked. No view through it reveals the outside.

The visual style is photorealistic Lovecraftian unease: damp timber, muted charcoal and brown, grey-green shadows and restrained warm light across the table. The opening is seen in first person from seated eye level. This atmosphere does not establish supernatural events, monsters or a cult as facts.

The back of your head aches. You do not remember arriving. Being struck and brought here is a possibility, not a confirmed fact. Neither your identity nor the reason for your arrival has been established.

The envelope and phone are the two investigative props. The door is the exit. The furniture provides atmosphere, not additional puzzles.
