Three days ago, a woman using a false name came to my surgery with an unpublished fourth illustration.

She made me promise not to reveal her hiding place. She also made me promise that if the third picture came true, I would find you at once.

It came true before dawn. Scotland Yard suspects her. The newspaper locks the fourth plate at noon.
