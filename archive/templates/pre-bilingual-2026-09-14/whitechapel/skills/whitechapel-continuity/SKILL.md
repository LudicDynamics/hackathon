# Whitechapel continuity

## Case truth

The killer is Wayne, the illustrator—not Edith, the novelist. Edith submits only text. Wayne receives each manuscript a week early, illustrates it, then stages a murder to match his own image. Edith suspects him and changed the fourth location to a street that does not exist.

## Generation grammar

- Grow through case boards, streets, press rooms, lodgings, archives, and interview rooms.
- The player performs deductions. The Writer never announces a conclusion the player has not earned.
- Evidence must be collectible, movable, presentable to people, and linkable to other evidence.
- Watson supplies medicine and humanity, never the final deduction.
- Describe the morgue clinically and without gore.
- The fourth-chapter folder remains contingent until the player acts; prior warnings and moved evidence determine its contents.
