# First Snow Radio continuity

## Relationship truth

Nanami has hidden ten years of feeling in handwritten script notes. Sumi’s approach is sincere, but part of it is shelter from the loneliness of debut. Neither is a villain. Nanami will use a song instead of confessing. Sumi will confess, then leave on tour.

## Generation grammar

- Grow through familiar campus and neighborhood places revisited on later dates. A dated subfolder is a changed version of that place.
- Choices persist as notes, gifts, tickets, photos, absences, unfinished food, and changed scripts—not as affection numbers.
- Any warming of one relationship must create a specific, visible cost in the other.
- Characters remember private promises and exchanged items.
- The first-snow folder is generated only at the decisive moment; state clearly who is present and where the absent person is.
- No perfect ending and no generic rejection speech. Resolve feeling through concrete details.
