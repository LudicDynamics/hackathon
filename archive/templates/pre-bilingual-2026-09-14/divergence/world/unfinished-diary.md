That night Ryo called while you were working late. The next morning, news came from Tokiwa Electrics. Fifteen years have passed. You still keep the wind-up frog she pressed into your hand: “When it breaks, bring it to the shop. Dad can fix anything.” The building comes down next month. You came back.
