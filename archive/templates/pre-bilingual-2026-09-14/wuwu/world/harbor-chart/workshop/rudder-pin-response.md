Vera’s laugh comes quick and bright. She props her violet arm on the bench and lowers her voice as if the workshop itself might gossip.

“The pin was swapped. The old one bore a crest under the grime. If you want the rest, look where the broken metal was set aside, not where the wheel keeps turning.”
