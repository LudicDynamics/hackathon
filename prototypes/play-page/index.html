Five ways to make a world worth entering.

These are independent visual directions for the complete play page. Each prototype keeps the same narrative responsibilities, but rethinks the furniture, rhythm, materials and attention path. The world itself remains a genuinely pannable, zoomable infinite canvas in every variant. Choose the direction that deserves production work.
